Kickz Caviar

How the portal works

Kickz Caviar offers different ways to make money and this guide walks you through all of them. It tells you: what you are looking at, what each button does, and what happens after you press it.

01 · BEFORE ANYTHINGGetting in

Never dealt with us before?

Press Sign up and fill in the form. We ask for your details and company info, because that is who we pay.

As soon as your profile exists you go to the Discord step below. Do not skip it. You can not deal with Kickz Caviar if your Discord is not linked.

Have you sold to us before?

Then you already have a profile, and it holds your history. You only need a password. Do not sign up again.

  1. On the login screen, press First time login or forgot password?
  2. Type your email first, then press it. You get a mail with a link.
  3. Set a password there and log in with it.

Signed up by mistake? The portal tells you the profile already exists and sends you back to this button. Nothing breaks.

Discord is required

You cannot trade without it. The portal will not let you claim, offer, accept or buy if your profile is not linked.

Our bots keep you updated throughout the entire deal process, and every deal you make automatically gets its own dedicated Discord channel for processing and communication. That’s why linking Discord is required to trade — no Discord, no deals.

  1. A window appears with a Link Discord button. Press it.
  2. Approve it on Discord's own screen.
  3. It links your Discord and redirects you back to the portal.

If you leave the server later, everything stops within seconds. You will get a Rejoin Discord button that rlinks your Discord in one press.

02 · THE HOME PAGESelling: your way to sell

The home page is the market. Everything there is something we want to buy from you.

The two switches at the top

  • Deal Type — Quick Deals or Want To Buys. Two ways to sell. Both are explained below.
  • Payout View — Margin / VAT21 or VAT0. This does not change the deal, only which payout you see. Set it to the scale you invoice in. The price on the card is the number you get.
Set this first. Always select the payout view that matches how you invoice. Using the wrong view means you'll be comparing offers against the wrong payout amount.

Finding a pair

  • Search takes a SKU or a product name and filters while you type.
  • All Brands narrows it to one brand.
  • Sort gives you newest, oldest, A–Z, Z–A, and payout low → high or high → low. Payout sorting follows the scale you picked above.
  • Cards / Table changes the layout. Table fits much more on screen when you scan a lot at once.

03 · QUICK DEALSClaim at a fixed payout

A Quick Deal is a pair we need, at a payout we have already set. Nothing to negotiate. The price is on the card, and the first one to claim it gets it.

The payout goes up over time.

Every card shows two amounts and a clock:

On the cardWhat it means
CurrentWhat you get if you claim right now
MaxThe highest it will ever go
3h 20m leftTime until it reaches Max

The payout increases from Current to Max over 12 hours, starting from the moment the deal appears. Once the maximum is reached, the timer will show Max payout reached and the payout will stop increasing.

Waiting means a higher payout, but also a higher chance that another seller claims the deal first. If you want to secure the sale, claim it early. If you're willing to risk losing the deal for a higher payout, you can wait.

Claiming

  1. Press Claim Deal.
  2. Pick your VAT type: Margin, VAT21 or VAT0. Select the VAT type that applies to the pair you're selling, as this determines your payout.
  3. Confirm.

A card that says Claim in Progress… is already being handled by someone else. Nothing is broken. It is just gone.

What happens next?

Your claim will appear under Quick Deals → Open Claims in your dashboard, and a dedicated Discord channel will automatically be created for the deal. You can open the channel directly from your dashboard or find it in our Discord server. From there it follows the chain.

04 · WANT TO BUYSName your own price

Want To Buys let you make your own offer. Set the price you want for the pair and the buyer can accept, decline or counter your offer. Other sellers can compete for the same WTB, while you manage the entire negotiation from your dashboard.

Making an offer

  1. Set Deal Type to Want To Buys.
  2. The card shows Current Offer— the best offer so far, or No offer yet. The amount is displayed according to your selected Payout View.
  3. Press Make Offer, select the VAT type that applies to your pair, and enter your offer. Your selected VAT type determines how your offer is getting processed.

Check the Payout View switch before you type a number. Your amount is read in the scale that is showing.

"No offer yet" does not mean any price will be accepted. Buyers can set a maximum price when posting a request. If your offer is above that limit, it will be declined immediately — even if you're the first seller to make an offer. You can simply try again with a lower offer; the WTB remains open.

Managing your offers

Go to Want To Buys → Offers. Here you'll find all the offers you've made and their current status. Use the three filters to see which offers are still open, have received a counter offer, or were denied but can still be renegotiated.

FilterWhat sits thereYour move
OpenAny offer still waiting for a response — whether it's your initial offer or a counter offer you've sentEdit the amount, Accept previous counter or Delete
CounteredThe buyer responded to your offer with a counter priceAccept theirs, Counter again, or Deny
DeniedAn offer or counter offer was declinedRetry at a new price, Accept previous counter or Delete

The columns show you exactly where each offer stands. Your Offer shows the last price you offered. Counter Offer shows the buyer's current counter when there is one, otherwise you'll see their Buyer's Last Offer. Current Lowest shows the best price currently offered by any seller, with a coloured dot indicating whether that offer is yours.

The green Accept button already shows the buyer's offer. If you're happy with their price, simply press it to accept.

Sent a counter offer? Your offer stays under Open while you wait for the buyer to respond. The Countered tab is only for counter offers you receive from the buyer.

While waiting, you can still accept the buyer's previous price. The green Accept button shows their last offer — press it at any time to accept and close the deal immediately.

A denied offer doesn't have to be the end. Retry lets you submit a new price, which the buyer can respond to like any other offer.

One negotiation, multiple sellers

The buyer doesn't negotiate with every seller separately. Your offer competes with the other offers, while the buyer negotiates through one central flow based on the best price available.

If the buyer sends a counter offer, every seller still in the running receives the same counter and can respond to it. This keeps everyone in the same negotiation without separate DMs or individual price discussions.

You compete with other sellers, but the negotiation stays in one place. Everyone receives the same buyer counter and decides whether to accept it, counter it, or walk away.

After accepting

Once a deal is accepted, it enters The Chain: Accepted, Confirmed, Label Requested, Ready To Ship, Shipped, and Delivered.

05 · CONSIGNMENTYour stock, available to the network

What it does

You list your stock at your own price and make it available across the Kickz Caviar network. Buyers can discover your pairs and make offers on them, while your listings are also automatically offered on matching Want To Buys when there is an opportunity.

When a deal matches your price, you still confirm the sale before it moves forward. This means your stock can actively reach demand across the network without you having to constantly watch Want To Buys or place offers yourself.

Your listings are also be published directly on the websites of online stores connected to us, making your pairs available to their customers as part of the store's inventory. One listing, multiple ways to sell.

Price matters. Your pair is only shown on a connected store's website when your asking price fits within that store's pricing. If it doesn't, the listing won't be shown there at all. A competitive price gives your stock more exposure and more opportunities to sell.

Consignment has to be switched on for your profile. Not a consignor yet? There is a Become a consignor link on your dashboard to apply.

List only what you can deliver

Consignment relies on accurate inventory. When you list a pair, buyers and connected stores treat it as available at the price you've set.

Keep your inventory up to date and only list pairs you can actually deliver. Repeatedly failing to confirm listed stock, or not honoring your listed price, may result in removal from the consignment network. Reliable listings are what keep the network working for everyone.

Adding one pair

  1. Consignment → Inventory, then + Add Stock.
  2. SKU — for example DM0879-100. Unknown codes are refused.
  3. VAT Type — Margin, VAT21 or VAT0. Select the VAT type that applies to the pair you're listing.
  4. Size EU, Selling Price, Quantity. One row per size. Add as many as you like under the same SKU.

Adding stock in bulk

  • Add Stock adds the quantities in the file on top of what you have.
  • Replace Stock deletes your whole inventory and puts the file in its place.

Press Download CSV Template. The columns are fixed:

The same SKU and size twice in one file gets you a warning before anything is saved.

Changing it later

  • Edit changes the selling price or the quantity of a row.
  • Delete removes the row.
  • The search box filters what is on screen: product name, SKU, size and brand at the same time.

Offers arrive on their own

This is what makes consignment different from Want To Buys. There you go looking and place an offer. Here the offers come to you. A store wants a pair you have listed, and it lands in Consignment → Offers.

From there it works like the Want To Buys offers tab: the same three filters, the same ACCEPT, Counter and Deny. After that comes the consignment chain, which is one step longer than the others, because you still have to confirm you have the pair.

The count in the sidebar goes up when something needs you.

06 · BUYINGSourcing pairs for yourself

Switch the home page to Buying and you see what is available to you, instead of what we want from you.

Finding a pair

Search by SKU or name, filter by brand, sort the same way as on the selling side. Buying Type sets the price scale you are shown: All Types, B2B Only or Margin Only.

B2B prices are shown without VAT and say so. Margin prices include it. Check which one you are looking at before you compare anything.

Opening a product

Click a product and you get every available size in one table. Five columns, and each one is worth reading:

ColumnWhat it tells you
SizeEU size
Lowest PriceCheapest one available in that size, in the scale you picked
ETAHow fast it ships. Normally 1–2 business days
SourcesHow many sellers can supply it
Available QtyHow many pairs there are in total

Sources is the column most people miss. A size with several sources is much more likely to actually arrive: if the first seller cannot deliver, we can ask the others. One source is one person's word.

Buy or Offer

  • Buy takes it at the listed price.
  • Offer lets you name a lower amount and see whether anyone takes it.

Neither one is final on the spot. The seller still has to confirm they really have the pair.

You are never leaning on one seller. Press Buy or Offer and we do not just forward it to the cheapest listing and hope. The same request goes out to every other seller who holds that size, and a want-to-buy is posted to the wider network at the same moment.

So if that one listing turns out to be old, others are already working on it. That is why buying here is worth more than a screenshot of somebody's stock list.

Not there? Post a Want To Buy

If nobody is listing what you need, put out a request and let sellers come to you. It goes to every matching seller in our network.

  1. Press Post WTBs, then + Single WTB. (+ Bulk WTB is the CSV route, below.)
  2. SKU and Size. A Max Price is optional: the most you are willing to pay. Sellers never see it. It only decides which offers reach you.
  3. Buying Type — choose which inventory types can match your Want To Buy and how your Max Price is interpreted: all inventory, B2B only, or Margin only.
A Max Price is a ceiling, not a promise. Filling one in does not agree to anything and does not buy anything. You still decide on every offer, one by one. Leave it empty and every offer comes through, and you counter them down yourself.

Sourcing a whole list? + Bulk WTB lets you upload a CSV with SKU, Size and an optional Max Price. Leave Max Price empty for any pair where you want to receive all offers, or leave the column out entirely. A CSV template is available to download.

07 · YOUR BUYING TABSTrack your requests and offers

Open WTBs

Buying → Open WTBs shows all your active Want To Buys. If you sourced a pair elsewhere or no longer need it, simply Delete the request. Active WTBs remain visible to sellers across the network, so keeping this list up to date prevents sellers from responding to pairs you no longer need.

Offers on your requests

Seller offers appear under Buying → Offers. Use the Open, Countered and Denied filters to keep track of each negotiation, and accept, counter or decline offers directly from your dashboard.

  • ACCEPT accepts the seller's offer at the price shown.
  • Counter sends the seller a counter offer.
  • Deny declines the seller's offer.

Payments

An accepted offer moves to Buying → Payment Due. A "!" appears next to the tab whenever a payment is waiting, so you can immediately see when something needs your attention.

  1. Press Pay and a Mollie payment page opens.
  2. Complete the payment to move the deal forward.

Each deal has its own payment link. If you close the payment page before completing it, you can simply use the Pay button again to reopen the same payment.

Your payment does not go directly to the seller. We hold the payment in escrow while the deal is being completed and only pay the seller once the deal has been successfully completed. This allows the seller to move forward while your payment remains protected throughout the process.

After payment, you'll receive a request to provide a shipping label once the seller is ready to ship. From there, The Buying Chain guides you through the remaining steps of the deal.

The DISCORD button

Every deal has its own Discord channel. Press DISCORD in the Actions column to open the channel linked to that specific deal.

Quick Deals. After claiming a deal, use its Discord channel to continue the deal and handle any deal-specific communication.

Want To Buys. Once your offer is accepted, use the deal's Discord channel to continue the deal and handle any deal-specific communication.

Packing. Use a clean shipping box without branding, remove any price stickers or unnecessary markings, and do not include anything extra in the package. Pack the pair securely and make sure the shoebox and product are well protected during transit.

09 · WORTH KNOWINGSmall things that save time

  • The sidebar counts are live. The number next to each tab shows how many deals are currently in that step of the process.
  • Your search stays active when switching tabs. If a tab looks unexpectedly empty, check the search box and clear it with the ×.
  • Set your views before comparing prices. Payout View controls how payouts are displayed when selling, while Buying Type determines which inventory types you see when buying.
  • Prices may sometimes include cents. Most offers are entered as whole euros, but VAT conversions can result in amounts such as €102.10. This is normal.
  • Sizes are shown in EU sizing. Brand-specific fractions are kept as they are, such as 42 2/3 instead of 42.67.
  • Choose the correct VAT type when selling. The VAT type should match the VAT treatment of the pair and directly affects how your payout is calculated.
  • Always respond to the current offer. When an offer is replaced by a newer one, the previous offer becomes inactive and is shown in grey.
  • Discord actions are connected to the portal. Accepting or responding to a deal through its Discord channel updates the same deal in your dashboard, so you do not need to repeat the action in the portal.
